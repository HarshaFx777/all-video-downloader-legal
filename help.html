Help

How do I download a video?

Open the in-app browser, navigate to a page with a video, and tap the download button once it's detected. Pick a quality on the next screen and tap "Start downloading."

Why didn't a video download?

Not every website is supported, and YouTube videos cannot be downloaded with this app.

Where do my downloads go?

Downloaded videos are saved to your device's Downloads folder and appear in the app's Download screen.

How do I play a video from the Video library?

Tap the "Video" banner on the home screen to see sample clips. Tap a thumbnail to play it, or tap the row to re-analyze its source link in the browser.
