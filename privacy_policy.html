Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Video Downloader app ("the app") handles information when you use it.

Permissions the app uses

The app may request the following permissions:

  • Internet access — to browse web pages and download videos you choose to save.
  • Storage / Downloads access — to save videos you download to your device.
  • Notifications — to send occasional re-engagement reminders, which you can disable in your device settings at any time.

Information we collect

The app does not require you to create an account and does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

The app uses third-party services that may collect data used to identify you, as described below:

  • Google AdMob — used to display ads within the app. AdMob may collect device identifiers, approximate location, and usage data to serve and measure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase (Google) — used for crash reporting, analytics, and remote configuration of app features. See Firebase's Privacy and Security policy.

How we use information

Any data collected by the third-party services above is used solely to operate the app, display ads, diagnose crashes, and improve app performance. We do not sell your personal information.

Data retention and deletion

We do not maintain our own user database. Data collected by third-party services (AdMob, Firebase) is retained and can be managed according to each provider's own policies. You can reset your advertising identifier or opt out of personalized ads in your device's settings.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the support channel listed on the app's store listing.
